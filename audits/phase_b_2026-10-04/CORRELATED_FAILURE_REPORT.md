# Correlated-failure analysis

This analysis does not count agreeing verdicts. An audit's source, arithmetic, normal form, parser, physical model, and receipt semantics are separate dependence layers. Distinct prompts/model labels do not prove statistical, conceptual, or implementation independence.

## Evidence families that remain correlated

- Author compact and full Gram checkers share their scalar arithmetic despite different certificate layouts. Historical author-side kill tests do not count as a separate arithmetic implementation.
- A01–A06 generally share the same frozen algebraic relations and interpret the same declared theorem. Different exact engines reduce software correlation, not theorem-definition correlation. Fresh literal-event and actual-embedding bridges are required at those edges.
- A01/A02/A03/A05/A06 common-space numerical tests are variants of canonical square-root dilation plus an orthogonal complement. A04's direct common Halmos construction is an alternative representation; its11d direct-sum variant reuses that implementation and is not an independent unitary-completion engine.
- Finite-field reductions verify algebraic specializations, never real positivity. Finite-field concrete matrices break normal-form dependence but cannot by themselves establish the characteristic-zero universal identity.
- Gram/LDL and compact SOS are linked certificate paths. Repeated copies of the same14 weights are not independent positivity evidence. Checking compact SOS suffices for the theorem but does not authenticate every redundant Gram byte.
- Numerical random searches, local optimization and SDP runs are diagnostics with their own feasibility and status limitations. None is used to replace exact SOS positivity and the universal bridge.
- Every software result still shares a Python/runtime/hardware trust boundary. Meta-replays reduce specific implementation risk; no proof assistant, external peer review or absolute correctness guarantee is claimed.

The exhaustive ledger and coverage matrix identify which apparent checks are excluded, corrected, duplicated or retained. Continued agreement after exclusions is a set of tested evidence, not a vote.
